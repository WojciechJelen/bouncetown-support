Support for Bouncetown on iPhone and iPad.

Need help, found a problem or have a suggestion? Parents and guardians can contact the developer by email.

Email Bouncetown support

wjelen30@gmail.com

When reporting a problem

Please include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, the stage number and what happened. A screenshot can help, but please remove any personal information first. Do not send a child's name, photo or other personal details.

How do I play?

Tap Play, choose an unlocked map stop, then tap Let's bounce! Drag left and right to guide the paddle. Clear all breakable blocks to build a town reward. The hourglass offers slow time once per attempt.

Does the game need internet?

The single-player game works offline. Optional Apple Game Center sign-in needs Apple's services, but you can play without signing in.

Where is progress saved?

Progress and settings are saved on your device. Bouncetown does not provide cloud saves or progress transfer between devices. Deleting the app can delete its progress. New game resets your progress after confirmation.

Sound and effects

Use the Music, Sound and Effects controls to change those settings. Turn Effects off for reduced visual effects.
