Privacy policy

Bouncetown · Effective October 15, 2026

Bouncetown is developed by Wojciech Jelen. This policy explains how information is handled in the iPhone and iPad game, in support emails and on these support pages.

Playing Bouncetown

Bouncetown is a single-player game designed to work offline. The game does not require a Bouncetown account. We do not operate advertising, behavioral tracking, developer analytics, chat or user-generated content in the game. This version has no in-app purchases.

Progress and settings

Stage progress, the current attempt and sound, music and effects settings are stored locally on your device. Bouncetown does not send this saved progress to a developer-operated server and does not provide cloud saves. You can reset progress using New game after confirming, or remove the app and its local data through your device settings. Your device's own backup and restore features are controlled by Apple.

Optional Apple Game Center

The game offers optional native Game Center sign-in. You can dismiss sign-in and continue playing. Apple handles Game Center accounts and authentication under its own terms and privacy policy. Bouncetown does not receive your Apple Account password and does not operate public leaderboards, achievements or cloud saves.

Contacting support

If you email wjelen30@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use those messages to respond to you and investigate the reported issue. Email is handled through Gmail and may be stored by the email providers involved in delivery. We keep support correspondence only as long as needed to resolve the request or meet applicable legal obligations. You can contact the same address to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to those obligations.

Children

The game does not ask children to provide personal details. Parents and guardians should handle support requests. Please do not include a child's name, photo, contact details or other personal information. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can address it and delete it where appropriate.

These support pages

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add advertising, analytics scripts, tracking cookies or contact forms. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to operate and secure its hosting service. See GitHub's privacy statement. External links and your email provider have their own privacy practices.

Sharing and choices

We do not sell personal information or use support messages for targeted advertising. We may disclose information when required by applicable law. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information you have provided to us. Contact the developer to make a request.

Policy updates

We will update this page if the game's practices change, including if a later version adds optional paid content. The effective date above identifies this version of the policy.
